HTX Perpetual — Strategy Backtest

EMA + candlestick patterns · retrace/rejection entry · S/R breakout gate · 2R target · ATR stop
VERDICT

On live HTX USDT-perpetual history (12 pairs × 4H/1D/1W, ~Apr 2021 → Oct 2026), the full strategy as configured is NOT profitable — the short side loses badly in this uptrend and drags the whole book negative (PF 0.85). But the LONG side has a clear, durable edge: ~45% winrate, profit factor 1.28, + 1,315R across ~8,500 closed longs — and long-only on 1D/1W is even stronger (49% winrate, PF 1.45). The takeaway: this engine is a long-bias strategy; trading it long-only, and filtering out the lower timeframes, is where the edge lives.

How this was measured

  • Data: live HTX USDT-perpetual klines (api.hbdm.com/linear-swap-ex/market/history/kline), 2,000 candles per pair/timeframe; 12 symbols × 4H/1D/1W, spanning roughly Apr 2021 → Oct 2026.
  • Signal: EMA 9/21 golden & death cross, and/or candlestick reversal patterns (hammer, shooting star, engulfing, piercing, dark cloud, morning/evening star, three soldiers/crows), trend-aligned.
  • Entry: wait for a pullback to the Fibonacci retrace (default 50%) of the swing; enter only on a rejection candle there (pin bar / engulfing). If a nearby support/resistance level is present, require breakout → retrace to the level → rejection before entering. Fill at the rejection candle close. Rejection entries only on 4H and higher.
  • Exit: stop at the swing extreme ± ATR(14) buffer (1.0×); target at 2R (risk–reward).
  • Accounting: every closed trade contributes its realised risk-multiple (R). Winrate = TP / (TP + SL). Expectancy = mean R. Profit factor = gross wins ÷ gross losses. Equity = cumulative R. 2,233 trades still open at the data end are excluded from winrate.
Reproduce anytime: node backtest/backtest.js → writes backtest/results.json.

Limitations & honest caveats

  • This is a statistical walk-forward on historical data, not a live guarantee. The long edge reflects the strong crypto uptrend in the sample period; it can fade or invert in a bear regime.
  • No cooldown / no position sizing: consecutive signals on the same swing are treated as separate trades, which inflates the trade count (19,356 entries) and overstates realism. A live trader takes one position at a time.
  • Realised average win (≈1.6R) is below the 2R target because the fill is at the rejection close, which is usually past the ideal level — genuine slippage. Fees and funding are not modelled; on high-frequency 4H trading they would further erode the thin 4H edge.
  • The short side is deeply negative in this sample. Using the engine long-only, or at least being very selective on shorts, is what the data supports.
  • Not financial advice. This is a research tool. Only ever trade money you can afford to lose.
